3. Email Communications & Unsubscribe Policy

When you submit this registration form, your email address is used to send an automated registration confirmation as well as important event announcements from our official coordinator address (kdcbapsbkk@gmail.com). 4. Data We Collect, Storage & Ethical Guidelines

  • Parent / Guardian Name, Email & WhatsApp Contact: Collected for registration verification, automated confirmation emails, real-time parent communication, and emergency reachability.
  • Child's Full Name & Age: Collected to organize children into age-appropriate games, activities, and volunteer-supervised groups.
  • Food Allergies & Health Notes: Collected to ensure dietary safety during festive prasad and lunch service.
  • Where & How Data is Stored: All information is transmitted directly into BAPS Bangkok's restricted administrative Google Sheet accessible only to authorized event coordinators. Data is never sold, shared, or distributed to third parties.
